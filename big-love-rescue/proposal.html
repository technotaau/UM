Roadmap

The five phases

Phases 1–3 are reflected in this preview. Phases 4–5 start after BLR's feedback.

01

Discovery & Brand Audit

Complete
Week 1

We studied the current Weebly site, BLR's Petfinder, Adopt-a-Pet, Facebook and Instagram presence, the adoption, cat and foster JotForms, and the donation channels.

  • Content inventory of all 6 existing pages
  • Brand extraction: logo, colors, voice
  • UX and technical audit (below)
  • Goals: more applications, fosters and donations
02

UX Strategy & Design System

Complete (sample)
Weeks 1–2

A new sitemap built around the three actions that save lives: Adopt, Foster, Donate. Plus a design system that turns the logo into a warm, modern brand.

  • Sitemap and user journeys (adopter, foster, donor)
  • Color, type, icon and photo direction
  • Reusable components: dog cards, steps, FAQ, donate panel
  • Mobile-first layouts
03

Website Design & Build

Preview live, awaiting feedback
Weeks 2–4

A fast, responsive, accessible static website. This preview shows the full upgraded experience across Home, Adopt, Foster, Donate, Happy Tails and About & Contact.

  • Hero with rescue dog photography
  • Filterable adoptable-dog gallery
  • Donation panel with impact messaging
  • Copy rewritten for clarity and warmth
04

Integrations, Content & SEO

Up next
Weeks 4–5

We connect the site to the tools BLR already uses, so volunteers update one place and the website stays current.

  • Live Petfinder feed for available dogs
  • JotForm applications embedded on-page
  • Recurring donations (e.g. Zeffy or Givebutter, both free for nonprofits) alongside PayPal, Venmo and Zelle
  • Real Happy Tails gallery, SEO, schema, Google Business Profile
  • WCAG 2.2 AA accessibility pass, analytics, Google Ad Grants readiness
05

Launch, Training & Growth

Planned
Week 6 onward

We move bigloverescue.org to the new site with zero downtime, train the volunteer team, and support growth with USHU Marketing.

  • Domain/DNS cut-over from Weebly, SSL, redirects
  • Short video training for volunteers
  • Monthly care plan: updates, backups, uptime
  • Campaign support: adoption events, giving days, social

Phase 2 design direction

Same heart, warmer look.

We kept everything BLR is known for: the bold black-and-white pit bull logo, the red heart, and the burgundy accent from the current site. We paired them with warm cream tones, friendly modern type and real dog photography. The voice stays BLR's own: honest, caring and direct ("we're looking for the right fit, not the fast one").

  • Headings: Bricolage Grotesque, bold and friendly like the logo
  • Body: DM Sans, highly readable on phones
  • Accent: Caveat handwriting, used sparingly for warmth
  • Photography: real BLR dogs first, with a dog in every hero
