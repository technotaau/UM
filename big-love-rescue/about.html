About us

Who we are

Big Love Rescue was formed in 2019 in Cypress, Texas, to help homeless animals in our community who wouldn't otherwise have a chance.

What we believe

Every life matters.

We're here to make sure no dog is forgotten.

We take in dogs from overcrowded shelters and desperate situations. We give them top-notch vet care, behavioral evaluations and training when needed, so each dog is ready for the right home. We also teach our community about responsible pet ownership and the importance of spaying and neutering.
