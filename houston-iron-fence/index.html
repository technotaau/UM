1. Summary

The short version

The website is technically well built. Every page loads, canonicals and redirects are set up correctly, each page has a unique title and the structured data is detailed.

What it lacks is local proof: reviews, real photos and linked listings. There are no customer reviews or photos of real jobs. Nothing links to a Google Business Profile or other listing, and there is no analytics. Google uses these signals to decide which local companies to show. In our test searches the site did not appear for any of the main iron fence and gate searches, while competitors with visible review counts did.

Most of the technical setup is in place. The biggest gains now come from adding real-world proof and tightening what already exists.

2. Strengths

What is working

Keep these as they are.

  • All 59 sitemap pages return status 200. Unknown pages correctly return 404.
  • Clean URLs. Missing trailing slashes and /index.html redirect to the right page in one step.
  • Every page has a self-referencing canonical tag and exactly one H1.
  • No duplicate titles or meta descriptions anywhere on the site.
  • XML sitemap listing all 59 main pages, referenced in robots.txt.
  • Detailed LocalBusiness, Service, FAQ and Article structured data with zero parsing errors.
  • Fast server: 50 to 100 ms response, HTTP/2, Brotli compression and a CDN.
  • Phone number 346-579-7200 is identical on every page.
  • 20 unique articles of about 910 words each on real buyer questions such as cost, permits and rust.
  • Multi-step quote form sends leads to a CRM, plus a sticky call bar on mobile.
3. Issues

Issues by priority

Ranked by how much each one limits leads from search. Effort is our estimate for a developer or marketer familiar with the site.

Critical

C1. No analytics or conversion tracking

CriticalEffort: 2 to 4 hours
Found
No Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager, Meta Pixel or similar tag on any page. No Search Console verification tag (it may be verified by DNS, which we cannot see).
Why it matters
There is no way to know how many people visit, which pages bring calls or whether any SEO work is paying off.
Fix
Install Google Analytics 4 through Tag Manager. Track phone link clicks and quote form submissions as conversions. Add call tracking to the main number. Verify Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools and submit the sitemap to both.

C2. No customer reviews anywhere

CriticalEffort: ongoing
Found
The reviews page says: "We don't have verified customer reviews published on this page yet." The homepage and all 16 city pages show a "What customers say" section with a similar placeholder message. A web search for the business name with reviews returned competitors only.
Why it matters
Reviews are one of the strongest factors for showing in Google Maps and the local results, and homeowners read them before they call. Competitors in the results show ratings such as 4.6 stars from 172 reviews (Griffin Fence).
Fix
Start a review routine: after each job, text the customer a direct link to leave a Google review. Aim for a steady flow each week rather than a burst. Show real reviews on the site once they exist and remove the placeholder sections until then.

C3. Business listing and address signals are weak

CriticalEffort: 1 to 3 weeks
Found
The structured data lists no profiles (sameAs is empty), so there is no link to a Google Business Profile, Facebook, Yelp or BBB. In our searches, no third-party listing showed this phone number.
The address, 12837 Louetta Rd, is a two-story office building with numbered suites (101 to 203) shared by a pharmacy, an insurance agency and other tenants. The site lists "1st & 2nd Floor" with no suite number.
The map on the About and Contact pages points to "Cypress, TX" in general, not to the business. Hours are listed as "Open 24 hours, 7 days a week".
Why it matters
Google only allows a Business Profile address where staff work during business hours. Shared or virtual office addresses are a common reason profiles get suspended. Without a verified profile, the business cannot appear in Google Maps results.
Fix
Confirm whether a Google Business Profile exists and who owns it. If no one works at the Louetta Rd office during business hours, set the profile up as a service-area business with the address hidden. If it is staffed, add the exact suite number everywhere. Use real working hours. Then build consistent listings on Bing Places, Apple Business Connect, Yelp, BBB, Angi, Houzz, Nextdoor and Facebook, and add all of them to sameAs. Embed the actual Business Profile map.

C4. Every image is a stock photo

CriticalEffort: 1 to 3 weeks of job photos
Found
All 56 images on the site load from Unsplash, a free stock photo library. Only 23 different photos are used and one gate photo appears 9 times. The project gallery states: "The images below illustrate the styles and finishes available, not specific completed addresses."
Why it matters
For a contractor, photos of real work are the clearest proof of experience. Google's quality guidelines put weight on first-hand experience, and many homeowners recognize stock photos. Original images can rank in Google Images and can be added to the Business Profile.
Fix
Photograph every job: before, during and after. Build the project gallery from real jobs with the area named (for example "Ornamental steel fence, Bridgeland, Cypress"). Replace hero images on the homepage and main service pages first. Host images on the site's own server in WebP format with descriptive file names.

High

H1. 13 broken internal links

HighEffort: 2 to 6 hours
Found
Pages link to 13 addresses that return "404 not found", including /request-estimate/ (linked from a call to action), /automatic-gate-repair/ (linked from 2 pages) and /iron-fence-painting/. The full list is in the appendix.
Why it matters
Broken links lose visitors and send Google's crawler to dead pages. Several of the missing pages cover services the site already lists, such as automatic gate repair and solar gate openers.
Fix
Point /request-estimate/ to the quote form. Build the missing service pages that match services the company actually offers. Remove the other links.

H2. City pages are near copies of each other

HighEffort: 2 to 4 weeks
Found
The 16 service-area pages have about 1,450 words of main text each, but on average 50% of that wording is identical (measured with 5-word phrase overlap after removing the shared form and footer). Every page uses the same headings with only the city swapped, for example "Iron Fence & Gate Problems We See in Katy" and "Iron Fence & Gate Problems We See in Conroe".
Why it matters
Google's spam policy describes pages that differ mainly by city name as doorway pages, and these can be demoted. They also give a homeowner in Katy no reason to believe the company has worked there.
Fix
Rewrite the top 5 or 6 markets (for example Cypress, Katy, The Woodlands, Spring, Houston and Memorial) with real local detail: jobs completed there, neighborhoods, HOA rules, permit notes and local reviews. Merge the other city pages into the service areas page until there is real work to show for them.

H3. Placeholder text on 19 pages

HighEffort: 1 to 2 hours
Found
Messages such as "We're in the process of collecting and publishing verified customer reviews" and "The photos on our project gallery are style and inspiration examples" appear on the homepage, all 16 city pages, the reviews page and the projects page.
Why it matters
To a visitor these sections read like an unfinished business. They also repeat the same text across 19 pages.
Fix
Hide these sections until there is real content to show, then bring them back with actual reviews and photos.

H4. Quote form is buried on phones and main buttons fail contrast

HighEffort: 2 to 4 hours
Found
On a phone the homepage opens with a block of about 130 words over a dark photo, and the quote form starts well below the first screen. The orange "Get Free Quote" and "Call" buttons (white text on #C97D2E) have a contrast ratio of 3.25 to 1, under the 4.5 to 1 accessibility minimum.
Why it matters
Many people looking for a fence contractor will be on a phone. A long intro pushes the form out of view, and low contrast makes the most important buttons harder to read outdoors.
Fix
Cut the hero text to one or two sentences and show a "Get my free quote" button and the phone number in the first screen. Darken the orange button color slightly (for example to #B45309, which reaches 5 to 1) or use dark text on it.

Medium

M1. Slow main image on mobile

MediumEffort: 4 to 8 hours
Found
Largest Contentful Paint (when the main content appears) is 3.1s on the homepage and 4.3s on the Katy page in mobile tests. Google Fonts block the first paint by about 0.9s, and hero images load from Unsplash's servers. City pages do not preload their hero image.
Fix
Host fonts and images on the site itself, preload the hero image on every page, serve WebP or AVIF in mobile sizes, and set a one-year cache on the versioned CSS and JS files (currently 7 days).

M2. Titles and descriptions cut off in search results

MediumEffort: 2 to 3 hours
Found
14 titles are longer than 60 characters and 14 descriptions are longer than 160 characters, so Google is likely to cut them off. The homepage description is 193 characters. 11 descriptions are under 120 characters and leave space unused. The "| Houston Iron Fence" ending uses 21 characters on every title.
Fix
Rewrite to about 50 to 60 characters for titles and 140 to 155 for descriptions. Lead with the service and city, and drop the brand ending where space is tight.

M3. No social sharing tags

MediumEffort: 1 to 2 hours
Found
None of the 59 pages has Open Graph or Twitter card tags.
Why it matters
When a page is shared by text, on Facebook or on Nextdoor, the preview may show no image or the wrong one. Referrals through these channels are common for home services.
Fix
Add og:title, og:description, og:image (a real project photo) and twitter:card to the page template.

M4. Thin "who we are" signals

MediumEffort: 3 to 5 hours
Found
The About page names no owner, team members or license numbers and has no team photos. The 20 articles are credited to "Houston Iron Fence" rather than a person, have no images and 11 of them share the same publish date.
Fix
Add the owner's name, photo and years in the trade. List the team, proof of insurance and any licenses or certifications. Credit articles to a named person with a short bio, add a real photo to each and keep dates accurate.

M5. Structured data gaps

MediumEffort: 1 to 2 hours
Found
The LocalBusiness markup has no sameAs profiles and no geo coordinates. Article markup has no image. FAQ markup is on 18 pages, but Google now shows FAQ results mainly for government and health sites, so it brings little visible benefit.
Fix
Add profile links and coordinates once listings exist, add images to articles and keep the hours in the markup identical to the Business Profile.

Low

L1. Redirects, security header and old URLs

LowEffort: 1 hour
Found
http://houstonironfence.com takes two redirects to reach the final page (http to https, then to www). There is no HSTS header. Google still has an old WordPress-style address indexed (/?page_id=329), which suggests an earlier site; it now shows the homepage with a canonical tag.
Fix
Redirect every variant to https://www. in one step and add an HSTS header. Check Search Console for old URLs that still receive links and redirect them to the closest new page.

L2. Heading order and repeated forms

LowEffort: 1 hour
Found
Some pages jump from H2 to H4. The homepage has three near-identical "Get Your Free Quote" headings because the quote block appears more than once.
Fix
Keep heading levels in order and use one main quote form per page with buttons that scroll to it.

7. Action plan

Five-phase action plan

Each phase builds on the one before it. Timelines assume the business can supply photos, reviews and listing access as we go.

Measure and fix the basics

Week 1
  • Install Google Analytics 4 and Tag Manager with call and form conversions (C1)
  • Verify Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools and submit the sitemap
  • Fix the 13 broken links and point /request-estimate/ to the form (H1)
  • Hide placeholder sections (H3), fix button contrast and the mobile hero (H4)
  • Single-step redirects, HSTS and social sharing tags (L1, M3)
  • Fix heading order and keep one quote form per page (L2)

Build the local foundation

Weeks 1 to 3
  • Claim, verify and fully set up the Google Business Profile with the right categories, services, service areas and real hours (C3)
  • Decide on the address: staffed suite or hidden service-area address
  • Create matching listings on Bing, Apple, Yelp, BBB, Angi, Houzz, Nextdoor and Facebook
  • Add all profiles and coordinates to the structured data and embed the real map (M5)

Add real proof

Weeks 2 to 6
  • Start the review routine after every job (C2)
  • Photograph every job and rebuild the project gallery with real work by area (C4)
  • Upgrade the About page with the owner, team, insurance and licenses (M4)
  • Show real reviews on the homepage and city pages

Upgrade the content

Weeks 4 to 8
  • Rewrite the top 5 or 6 city pages with local jobs and details, and merge the other city pages into the service areas page (H2)
  • Expand the main cost page beyond its current 277 words
  • Rewrite long titles and descriptions (M2)
  • Self-host images and fonts and preload hero images, with a target of under 2.5s on mobile (M1)
  • Add photos and a named author to every article

Grow authority and leads

Month 3 onward
  • Build the missing service pages the site already links to: automatic gate repair, gate access control and solar gate openers
  • Earn local links from HOAs, suppliers, sponsorships and local chambers of commerce
  • Pitch the Houston cost guide to local blogs, HOAs and news sites for links
  • Monthly report on calls, form leads, rankings and reviews

How we tested

  • Crawled all 59 URLs in the XML sitemap and checked every internal link found on them (16 extra URLs: 13 broken, plus the accessibility, privacy and terms pages, which load fine).
  • Read the HTML of each page for titles, descriptions, headings, canonicals, images, links, structured data and word counts.
  • Measured duplicate content with 5-word phrase overlap between pages in the same group.
  • Ran Google Lighthouse 12 four times: the homepage (mobile and desktop), the Katy city page and a service page (mobile).
  • Checked redirects, response headers, robots.txt, the sitemap and old URL formats.
  • Searched the main buying terms, the business name, phone number and address.

Limits of this audit: we did not have access to Google Search Console, the Google Business Profile or a backlink tool, and the site has no analytics installed. Index coverage, real traffic, Maps rankings and backlinks are not measured here. Search results vary by location and device. Lab speed tests can differ from real visitor data.
